Party on the Path

Sunday, October 6, 2024 @ 6:00PM — 9:00PMSun, Oct 6, 2024 @ 6:00PM — 9:00PM

Eclipse De Luna, 764 Miami Circle NE, Atlanta, GA 30324

Livable Buckhead’s annual Party on the PATH celebrates art and greenspace, two areas of our work to make Buckhead an even better place to live, work and play. On October 1st, hundreds will join us for hot music, cool drinks and dancing under the stars at Eclipse di Luna Buckhead. The event kicks off with a stroll of Atlanta’s leading art galleries at Miami Circle, followed by an interactive, artist-led fiber art project, and a silent auction. Then get ready for an evening filled with award-winning Spanish-style tapas, icy mojitos and electrifying dancing to the sizzling sounds of 3rd Stream Big Band, who perform an explosive mix of Rock, Soul, R&B, Jazz and Latin beats!
